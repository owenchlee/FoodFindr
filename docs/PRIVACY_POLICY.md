## What this covers

This policy describes what FoodFindr ("the app") collects, why, and who it's
shared with. FoodFindr is a restaurant recommendation app: you tell it your
location, budget, and cravings, and it picks one restaurant and one dish for
you, using Google's Places data and Anthropic's Claude AI.

## Information we collect

### Account information
If you create an account, we collect your **email address** and a
**password**. Your password is never stored in plain text — it's hashed
with scrypt (a one-way cryptographic function) before it touches our
database, so we don't have access to your actual password and can't
retrieve it if lost. You can also use the app as a guest without an account,
which skips the features listed under "Account-only features" below.

### Location
When you allow location access (or manually search/drop a pin), we use your
location to find restaurants near you and to show how far away they are.
Your location is sent to our backend server, which forwards it to the
**Google Places API** to search for nearby restaurants and to the
**Google Geocoding API** if you search by address. We do not collect your
location in the background — only when the app is open and actively
searching.

If you have an account, we also keep a record of which cities you've
searched in and which restaurants have shown up in your results, to power
the "how much of your city have you explored" progress feature. This is
tied to your account, not to a precise location history.

### Search and preference data
The dish you're craving, your budget, group size, cuisine and dietary
preferences, and spice tolerance are sent to our backend, which forwards
your filtered restaurant list and preferences to the **Anthropic Claude
API** to generate a personalized recommendation and reasoning. If you have
an account, we also store your cuisine/dietary/spice preferences and the
"flavor tags" from restaurants you've rated, so future recommendations can
match your taste.

### Account-only features
If you create an account, you can additionally:
- **Log visits**: the restaurant name, dish, your star rating, and when you
  logged it. This also powers streaks, badges, and an opt-in leaderboard
  visible to other users of the app.
- **Join or create groups**: a group name and join code, so a group of
  friends can search together. Other group members can see that you're a
  member; we don't share your email address with them.

### What we don't collect
We don't use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We don't track
you across other companies' apps or websites. We don't sell your data.

## Who we share data with

- **Google (Places API, Geocoding API, Maps JavaScript API)** — receives
  your location (and search text, if you search by address) to find nearby
  restaurants and render the map. Subject to
  [Google's own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Anthropic (Claude API)** — receives your filtered restaurant list,
  budget, group size, dietary/cuisine preferences, and craving/dish search
  text to generate a recommendation. Subject to
  [Anthropic's own privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- We do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not sell
  your data to anyone.
